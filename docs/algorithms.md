# Algorithms

Let $E$ be an elliptic curve over ℚ.

If $E$ has 2-torsion, we use descent via 2-isogeny \[[Cre97](#cre97), §3.5\]. Let $\phi: E \to E'$ be a 2-isogeny. Then, descent via $\phi$ gives $\mathrm{rank}~E(\mathbb{Q}) \le s + s' - 2$, where $s$ and $s'$ are the dimensions of the two Selmer groups. `rankbound` then lowers this bound by the ranks of the Cassels pairings on the two Selmer groups \[[Fis17](#fis17)\], and, where that is not enough, by the rank of the Cassels–Tate pairing on the 2-Selmer group of $E$ or $E'$ \[[Fis22](#fis22)\]. If all the points of order 2 are rational, the least of the bounds from the three 2-isogenies is taken, and the bound drops by one when its parity disagrees with the root number. This last step is valid by the 2-parity theorem \[[Mon96](#mon96), [DD11](#dd11)\].

For curves without 2-torsion, we use the method described in \[[KSW19](#ksw19)\]. Let $F_E$ be the cubic field generated by a root of the 2-division polynomial of $E$. Then, by the Brumer–Kramer bound,

$$\mathrm{rank}~E(\mathbb{Q}) \le \dim \mathrm{Sel}_2(E) \le \dim \mathrm{Cl}(F_E)[2] + c$$

for a computable constant $c$, so it suffices to bound $\dim \mathrm{Cl}(F_E)[2]$. Assuming the GRH, the class group of $F_E$ modulo squares is spanned by the
ideal classes of the $N$ prime ideals of $F_E$ with norm below an explicit bound \[[BDF08](#bdf08)\]. Thus,
$\dim \mathrm{Cl}(F_E)[2]$ is at most $N$, so we obtain $\mathrm{rank}~E(\mathbb{Q}) \le N + c$.

To lower this bound, `rankbound` searches for *relations*, or pairs of integers $(a, b)$ such that for a fixed
generator $\alpha$ of $F_E$, $(a - b\alpha)$ factors into these $N$ prime ideals. Since $(a - b\alpha)$ is
principal, the exponents of its factors modulo 2 give a linear relation among their classes modulo squares. Given $d$
independent relations, we have $\mathrm{rank}~E(\mathbb{Q}) \le N - d + c$. Oftentimes but not always, this algorithm gives a sharp upper
bound on $\mathrm{rank}~E(\mathbb{Q})$.

If the discriminant of $F_E$ is small enough, we can bound ranks of curves without $2$-torsion unconditionally
using `rankbound bound --uncond`. This method relies on Minkowski's bound in place of that of \[[BDF08](#bdf08)\].
